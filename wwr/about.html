Whitewater Rafting

Our purpose is to share the thrill of the river with every guest. We believe that time on the water builds courage, teamwork, and lasting memories. Our experienced guides put safety first so that every adventure is as worry-free as it is unforgettable.

History

Whitewater Rafting began in 1998 with two guides, a single raft, and a bold idea: make the river accessible to everyone. What started as weekend trips for friends soon grew into a full outfitter serving families and thrill-seekers alike. Today our team of certified guides carries on that tradition, pairing local knowledge with a deep respect for the water.
